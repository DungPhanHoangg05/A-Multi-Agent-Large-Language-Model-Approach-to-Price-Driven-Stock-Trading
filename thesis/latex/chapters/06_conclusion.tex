\chapter{Conclusions and Future Work}\label{s:3929b96346005341}
\section{Approach and contributions}\label{s:fec2d201a3e32bcc}
This thesis integrates a daily price-driven multi-agent pipeline with
regime-aware historical evidence and numerical multi-task inference for
Vietnamese-stock LONG/CASH decisions. Specialist price/chart reports,
adapted alpha and dated sentiment form the current evidence; mature
episodes or marked task datasets supply context. Fact Anchor and an
external utility policy distinguish a hosted proposal from the final
action. Shared upstream, canonical net-cycle accounting and a forecast
barrier make the recorded path inspectable.

The contribution is this adaptation, context/policy integration and
source-bound evaluation. QuantAgent-style roles, indicators, Alpha101,
HMMs, Bayesian updating and multi-task ICL remain attributed foundations.
The local teacher/student architecture and objective are adaptations,
not claims to replicate the reference model or transfer its guarantees.
Implementation and reproducibility are contributions even where the
empirical comparisons do not establish a performance benefit.

\section{Answers to the research questions}\label{s:72579e525074c634}
\paragraph{RQ1: combined information and controls.}
Component Full exceeds Base by 3.247 pp BA on 102 points, with saved
descriptive interval [$-1.176$,8.125] pp, while its mean net account
return is 0.047 pp lower. Both identify seven of 25 UP outcomes; price
logistic has higher BA than Full. Separate numerical and nine-unit
factorial panels have different supports and prompt families. The
supported result is a limited classification difference with adverse
financial outcomes, not a general alpha--sentiment or LLM-selector benefit.

\paragraph{RQ2: historical context.}
The v1-minus-Common return difference is +0.327 pp [0.067,0.586], while the v1-minus-Native difference is
$-0.045$ pp [$-0.362$,0.271], within the complete twenty-cell descriptive
family. Its shorter-block Common band crosses zero. C2 and B each obtain
only three new Decision replies and otherwise mostly inherit Common.
All eight research-role means are negative. Outcomes depend on baseline,
readiness and dependence assumptions; no isolated regime effect or
prospective profitability is established.

\paragraph{RQ3: factual anchoring and action revision.}
MT and Fact Anchor agree in 302/306 final slots: 108 bypasses, 189
same-action proposals, five rejected changes and four accepted changes.
The MT--anchor return/BA bridge is +0.033/+0.433 pp, while MT's mean return
remains negative. This combined path does not isolate the student,
target context or utility benefit. A changed proposal and a changed final
action are different events; enum-valid facts do not certify all prose.

\paragraph{RQ4: contract evidence.}
The records support particular source-bound checks of economic labels,
pairing, forecast/outcome separation, prompt/retry/checkpoints and cold
artifact replay. Native transformations exhibit historical-value changes;
the checks do not establish full-system historical causal validity.
Provenance, execution and browser acceptance require further evaluation. These checks neither
freeze external hosted weights nor establish live trading readiness.

\section{Limitations and future work}\label{s:ff798c832c8c6036}
The main limits are six already exposed dependent folds, purposive
stock selection and narrow bank coverage; unavailable sentiment and
unauthenticated historical source availability; bank/query return-basis
differences and missing original signal journals; coupled prompt/input
interventions and missing matched ablations; and reference fractional
accounts without validated market execution or shared capital. Three
Decision repeats do not independently resample upstream or markets.
An accepted revision can lose, and a rejected LONG can miss a gain.

Priority work is to repair and test Native row-wise causality and
reconcile provenance in a separately versioned study, preserving the
documented historical limitations. Broader authenticated price/news/context coverage
and independent chart/fact-grounding checks should precede stronger
claims. Registered matched interventions must separate retrieval,
regime/statistics, prior/target tasks, numerical student and external
utility. A prospectively collected unexposed evaluation needs appropriate
dependence/precision planning and independently regenerated upstream
realizations. Venue-specific lots, fills, capital and settlement modeling,
then browser acceptance, remain distinct work. The present results
support a bounded historical assessment and identify these evidence
needs; they do not demonstrate profitable prospective deployment.
