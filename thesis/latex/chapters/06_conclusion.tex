\chapter{Conclusions and Future Work}\label{s:3929b96346005341}

\section{Summary of the approach}\label{s:fec2d201a3e32bcc}

This thesis connects a daily price-driven multi-agent analysis pipeline
with regime-aware historical context and numerical multi-task inference
for Vietnamese-stock LONG/CASH decisions. Price/volume features, chart
reports, adapted alpha signals and dated sentiment provide the current
evidence. Historical branches add earlier completed episodes, with
strict outcome-maturity requirements, market-state conditioning and
explicit readiness rules. The intended information boundary excludes
the current query's future outcome from forecasting.

The implementation provides several ways to introduce that history:
bounded Bayesian count summaries, conditional or alternative priors,
and numerical historical tasks for MT-BICL. The MT path combines a
factual anchor, a numerical student/teacher resource, a hosted proposal
and a utility-based revision policy. Its final action can retain the
anchor even when a new proposal is present. Section 4.8 details the numerical
context and its interface with the hosted decision stage.

The common economic contract is the candidate-LONG rule defined in §3.2
and introduced in §1.2, including the entry/exit schedule, two-sided costs
and SHORT-to-CASH mapping. Forecasts, policy actions and
future outcomes remain separate records, and paired context branches
reuse the same upstream realization. These choices make the recorded
comparisons inspectable; they do not resolve every inherited causal or
data-provenance limitation.

The contribution lies in the adaptation, context/policy integration and
evaluation described in Chapters 3--5. Chapter 2 attributes the inherited
multi-agent architecture, indicators, HMM/Bayesian foundations and
student--teacher methodology. The two historical studies evaluate this
particular implementation under different response collections and
statistical conventions. The answers below retain that separation and
summarize the already reported results, rather than adding experiments
or treating implementation as evidence of effectiveness.\footnote{This chapter synthesizes Chapter 5, especially B05a--b, B06a--e, the policy decomposition in §5.8.2 and B09. Evidence ledger §12.3/CL221--CL226 maps the answers to CL145--CL187 and the unchanged archived sources. No fitting, new model calls, resampling or execution replay is introduced.}

\section{Answers and contributions}\label{s:72579e525074c634}

The four answers follow the questions introduced in §1.3 and the
observation--answer--remaining-evidence map in Table B09 (§5.9.3). Percentage
point differences below retain the estimators and support specified in
Chapter 5; they are not returns on a validated multi-stock portfolio.

\subsection{RQ1: combined information and controls}\label{s:7cba3c757c28c519}

\textbf{How does the multi-agent system combining price, alpha and sentiment behave relative to its controls?}

The recorded component system shows a classification gain over Base
without a corresponding financial gain. On 102 paired points, Full has
BA of 52.961\% versus 49.714\%, a +3.247 pp difference; the saved six-block
descriptive interval is {[}\ensuremath{-}1.176, 8.125{]} pp.~Both identify only 7 of the
25 UP outcomes. Full's mean net account return is \ensuremath{-}0.797\% versus
\ensuremath{-}0.750\%, giving a \ensuremath{-}0.047 pp difference. The price-logistic control has
higher BA than Full, and CASH remains at zero return (§5.6.1).

The separate numerical and nine-unit factorial panels answer narrower
questions on their own support (§§5.6.3--5.6.4). They do not establish
an isolated benefit from the language model's factor selector or from
adding alpha and sentiment. Full/Base changes prompt representation
along with information. The supported answer is thus an observed,
representation-dependent classification difference with adverse
financial outcomes, not a general advantage of the combined system.

\subsection{RQ2: historical context and conditional evidence}\label{s:f4addef761acfc2d}

\textbf{How does regime-aware historical context and conditional evidence change outcomes under the same upstream realization?}

The outcome depends on the baseline and on whether contextual evidence
is actually available. Bayesian v1 exceeds Common by +0.327 pp in mean
account return, with saved joint descriptive band {[}0.067, 0.586{]} pp.
Against Native its difference is \ensuremath{-}0.045 pp, with band {[}\ensuremath{-}0.362, 0.271{]} pp
(§5.7.4). These belong to the complete twenty-cell contrast family, not
two independently chosen tests. The shorter-block L1 band for v1 minus
Common crosses zero (§5.8.4). The six exposed folds and dependence
assumptions prevent a confirmatory superiority interpretation.

Conditional branches are sparsely exercised: C2 and B\_semantic each
produce only three new Decision replies, while their remaining slots
retain designated fallback (§5.7.2). Primary comparisons include those
slots; covered subsets do not replace them. All eight context research
methods have negative mean net account returns (§5.7.1). The results
therefore support baseline- and readiness-dependent descriptive changes,
without isolating a regime effect or demonstrating profitability.

\subsection{RQ3: factual anchoring, MT inference and utility}\label{s:861a5e2202d28f5c}

\textbf{How do Fact Anchor, MT-BICL and the utility policy affect final actions?}

The combined MT path mostly retains the factual anchor. MT-BICL and
Fact Anchor agree on 302 of 306 final-action slots. The recorded
decomposition is 108 bypasses, 189 raw proposals equal to the anchor,
five rejected changes and four accepted changes (§5.8.2). Accepted
changes cover only three unique market points. This explains why a
new hosted reply and a different final action are different events.

The MT-minus-anchor bridge has +0.033 pp mean account-return difference
and +0.433 pp BA difference (§5.7.5), while MT's mean return remains
negative. The bridge changes combined mechanisms and supplies no
isolated student, target-context or utility-policy effect. Missing
Plain\_revision, No\_target, No\_utility and student--teacher interventions
remain missing (§5.9.1). The supported contribution is a traceable
anchor-relative revision path with very limited final-action changes,
not a measured benefit attributable to the student alone.

\subsection{RQ4: temporal, economic and operational evidence}\label{s:d546dd6b9caa558d}

\textbf{Which temporal, economic and operational contracts are supported by evidence, and which limitations remain?}

The frozen records support specific engineering contracts: canonical
candidate-LONG labels and cost accounting, shared upstream inputs,
forecast/outcome separation, measured prompt bounds, retry/checkpoint
handling and source-bound offline replay. Each conclusion applies to
the tested historical path and receipt scope (§§5.1, 5.3 and 5.8--5.9).
Reading a saved reply or reproducing an artifact introduces no new
hosted inference and cannot verify future served-model behavior.

The Native row-wise causality gate remains \textbf{FAIL} and the whole-system
status remains \textbf{NOT\_CERTIFIED\_HISTORICAL\_EXPLORATORY}. Bank/query/news
provenance, independent execution evidence and UI/F11 acceptance remain
unresolved; UI/F11 is \textbf{PENDING}. These limits prevent an assertion of
whole-system causal validity, live execution readiness or prospective
profitability. The answer to RQ4 is consequently a scoped inventory of
supported contracts and remaining failures, not overall certification.

Together, the answers distinguish four contributions. System adaptation
is supported by the implemented graph and component records;
historical-context/policy design by the specified mechanisms and traces;
evaluation/reproducibility by the paired accounting and scoped receipts;
and empirical findings by the two bounded historical studies. The
contribution types introduced in §1.5 are supported within these limits. None
requires turning a negative result into a trading advantage.

\section{Limitations}\label{s:ff798c832c8c6036}

\textbf{Temporal construction and source provenance limit the validity of the
historical conclusions.} The Native row-wise causality failure remains
unresolved. An outer cutoff at the query date cannot establish that
earlier rows were computed causally, and the Common alpha projection
does not certify every inherited feature or resource. The signal bank
and query prices also have different provenance. The audit finds
numerical return differences in 828 of 852 bank records; this is not a
count of changed labels. The unavailable original raw signal journals
prevent complete independent reconstruction of signal generation.
Membership hashes identify stored records but cannot authenticate their
original production. Historical news dates, adjusted prices, corporate
actions, volume units and point-in-time membership are likewise not
independently verified throughout the collection (§5.9.2). These gaps
affect the interpretation of the existing results, not merely the
convenience of reproducing them.

\textbf{The empirical support is small, dependent and previously exposed.}
The purposive nine-stock sample and six examined temporal folds do not
provide an untouched evaluation of the wider Vietnamese market. BHN's
shorter eligible history further limits uniformity. Three Decision
repeats share one upstream realization at each point; they do not test
independent chart-report variability. The component and context studies
have separate responses and statistical conventions. Their bootstrap,
joint-band and sensitivity constructions are descriptive; the L1/L2
difference shows why a favorable band cannot be treated as a stable
confirmatory result. Hosted aliases also leave served-weight identity
and foundation-model historical training overlap unresolved. Local
source and weight hashes cannot answer those external questions.

\textbf{Coverage and combined interventions limit mechanism attribution.}
Missing news and incomplete historical context change what the system
can use. Unavailable sentiment is not neutral sentiment, and a fallback
is not a new contextual model response. Sparse conditional activation
means that a full-cohort comparison may mostly describe retained
baseline actions. Full/Base and Native/Common also change representation
or instructions along with information. The small factorial panel and
the Common--Anchor--MT bridge do not fill the missing matched comparisons
listed in §5.9.1. Numerical assertions inspect consistency with a fact
packet; they do not independently validate the financial interpretation
of every report. The observed MT/anchor agreement therefore does not
identify whether the student, context, teacher or utility gate would be
responsible for a different result under another protocol.

\textbf{Economic and application evidence stop short of deployment.} The
reference ledger uses daily bars and separately capitalized stock--fold
accounts. It does not establish available liquidity, queue priority,
actual partial fills, market impact or jointly feasible portfolio
funding. The twelve saved execution scenarios apply hypothetical
assumptions to fixed predictions; they do not constitute certified fills
or guaranteed worst-case bounds. Zero CASH return belongs to that
reference contract. Lower drawdown or lower exposure does not remove
the negative research-method returns. Finally, the realtime interface
and the author's research-snapshot statement do not establish browser
acceptance or profitable prospective operation. UI/F11 remains PENDING;
external review and submission acceptance are separate activities.

These limitations leave a useful but bounded outcome: a traceable
implementation and an exploratory account of how its recorded decisions
change. They restrict causal, financial and deployment claims together.
The next research steps must add the specific evidence that is missing.

\section{Future work}\label{s:49fa8b2e540afbae}

The following priorities follow the dependencies among validity,
attribution and external evaluation. They are proposed work, not
completed repairs, additional results or retrospective changes to the
locked studies.\footnote{Limitations and missing interventions are carried from §§5.8--5.9/B06h/B09 and evidence ledger §12.4/CL227--CL230. Priority order is a research recommendation derived from those dependencies, not evidence that any listed experiment, repair or acceptance activity has been completed.}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\item
  \textbf{Repair temporal construction and establish provenance.} A separately
  versioned study should first make Native feature construction causal
  at every row, with explicit fit periods and resource identities. Bank
  and query inputs should be reconciled against authenticated source
  series, original signal journals and date-specific news availability.
  Label generation must continue to use the common economic oracle and
  exclude examples whose exits are not strictly before the cutoff.
  Evidence should include row-wise prefix-invariance checks, cutoff and
  maturity violations that fail explicitly, complete source manifests
  and an audit of bank/query return differences. This would establish
  what the repaired study can claim. It must not overwrite frozen
  predictions or reclassify the current Native FAIL as a historical pass.
\item
  \textbf{Improve and validate usable context coverage.} On the repaired
  foundation, expand the authenticated historical bank and dated news
  coverage, then measure readiness and missingness across stocks, dates
  and regimes. Chart/report grounding needs independent checks of
  reported features; sentiment needs validation for the financial domain
  and the actual news-selection path. Wider coverage is useful only if
  its eligibility and meaning are verified. The required evidence is a
  coverage audit with source availability, example maturity, fallback
  reasons and domain-validation results, retaining the full-cohort
  endpoint alongside clearly secondary covered-subset analyses.
\item
  \textbf{Identify mechanisms with registered matched interventions.} Hold
  the bank, feature projection, prompt format/budget and support fixed
  while comparing random, recent, similarity and Bayesian retrieval,
  and statistics-only, examples-only and no-regime conditions. For MT,
  include the missing plain-revision, no-target, no-utility and
  student--teacher comparisons. Record raw proposals and final actions
  separately so that retrieval, numerical inference and policy effects
  can be distinguished. Each comparison should share its upstream
  realization; independent upstream repetitions should additionally
  measure upstream variability. Predeclared contrasts, support rules,
  endpoint families and uncertainty procedures would make the resulting
  attribution stronger than the present combined bridges. Implemented
  builders alone cannot supply that evidence.
\item
  \textbf{Evaluate a fixed protocol on previously unexposed periods.} After
  repairs and design decisions are frozen, collect a prospective or
  otherwise genuinely unexposed evaluation with source/model/time
  identities and interruption records. Plan its duration and precision
  around dependent temporal support, rather than counting repeated
  prompts as independent market samples. Retain CASH and numerical
  controls, unfavorable outcomes, missing-context paths and all
  predeclared contrasts. Classification, exposure and net account
  economics should remain separate endpoints. Such evidence is needed
  to assess transfer beyond the exposed folds; choosing a different
  retrospective interval or favorable subset cannot provide it.
\item
  \textbf{Validate execution, portfolio constraints and application behavior.}
  Deployment research requires venue- and date-specific evidence for
  trading units, auctions, costs, settlement, liquidity and fill behavior.
  A capital-constrained multi-stock ledger should explicitly model
  overlapping positions, available cash and reconciliation before its
  return is described as a portfolio outcome. Application acceptance
  should verify the displayed branch, source identity, prompt/policy
  path, fallback/error states and checkpoint recovery against recorded
  results, including the three modules covered by the author's snapshot
  statement. Completing UI/F11 would establish the tested application
  behavior; evidence of prospective execution and profitability would
  still require its own evaluation.
\end{enumerate}

The current thesis ends with these evidence requirements open. Its
implemented mechanisms and historical findings provide a basis for that
work, while the causal failure, adverse financial outcomes and unresolved
deployment limits remain part of the record.
