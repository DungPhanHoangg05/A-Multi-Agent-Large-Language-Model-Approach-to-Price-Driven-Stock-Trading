\chapter{Conclusions and Future Work}\label{s:3929b96346005341}
\section{Approach and contributions}\label{s:fec2d201a3e32bcc}
This thesis integrates specialist price/chart reports, adapted alpha and
dated sentiment with regime-aware historical context and numerical
multi-task inference for Vietnamese-stock LONG/CASH decisions. Fact Anchor
and an external utility policy separate hosted proposals from final
actions. Shared upstream, canonical net-cycle accounting and a forecast
barrier make the recorded paths inspectable.

The thesis tests whether BICL adds value to Our system. In the follow-up
evaluation, BICL v1--v4 produce differences of at most $0.0127$ pp in
mean account return against Our system (Common) on the complete-account
subset. BICL v5 has lower return and BA than both no-context baselines
and Fact Anchor. BICL changes the information and action path when
eligible, yet the measured outcomes do not establish a consistent
economic benefit. The earlier studies below show how comparison results
vary with the input profile and available historical support.

The contribution is an attributed adaptation, context/policy integration
and source-bound evaluation of existing foundations: QuantAgent-style
roles, indicators, Alpha101, HMMs, Bayesian updating and multi-task ICL.
The local teacher/student model adapts the reference approach; its
empirical value is assessed within the reported limits.

\section{Answers to the research questions}\label{s:72579e525074c634}
\paragraph{RQ1: combined information and controls.}
Component Our system exceeds QuantAgent by 3.247 pp BA on 102 points, with saved
descriptive interval [$-1.176$,8.125] pp, while its mean net account
return is 0.047 pp lower. Both identify seven of 25 UP outcomes; price
logistic has higher BA than Our system. Separate numerical and nine-unit
factorial panels have different supports and prompt families. The
supported result is a limited classification difference with adverse
financial outcomes, not a general alpha--sentiment or LLM-selector benefit.

\paragraph{RQ2: historical context.}
In the earlier historical study, BICL v1 returns +0.327 pp [0.067,0.586] relative
to Our system (Common) and
$-0.045$ pp [$-0.362$,0.271], within the complete twenty-cell descriptive
family. Its shorter-block Common band crosses zero. BICL v2 and v4 each obtain
only three new Decision replies and otherwise mostly inherit Common.
All eight research-role means are negative. Regime awareness is implemented,
but its isolated benefit and robustness across all regimes have not been
demonstrated. Signal-bank queries cover BULL, CHOPPY and CONSOLIDATION
over only twelve dates, with no BEAR observations; MT query-state coverage
is not established. A matched comparison with and without regime matching
on the same points is needed to isolate its contribution.

\paragraph{RQ3: factual anchoring and action revision.}
BICL v5 and Fact Anchor agree in 302/306 historical final slots: 108 bypasses, 189
same-action proposals, five rejected changes and four accepted changes.
The BICL v5--anchor return/BA bridge is +0.033/+0.433 pp, while MT's mean return
remains negative. This combined path does not isolate the student,
target context or utility benefit. The student is an implemented and
approximation-verified component; these results do not establish a trading
improvement over direct teacher calculation. A changed proposal and a
changed final action are different events; enum-valid facts do not certify all prose.

\paragraph{RQ4: contract evidence.}
Recorded checks cover economic labels, pairing, forecast/outcome
separation, prompt/retry/checkpoints and cold artifact replay.
Our system transformations exhibit historical-value changes;
the checks do not establish full-system historical causal validity.
Provenance and execution require further evaluation. These checks neither
freeze external hosted weights nor establish live trading readiness.

\section{Limitations and future work}\label{s:ff798c832c8c6036}
The main limits are six exposed folds, narrow bank coverage, sparse news evidence, and uncertainty about when
historical data first became accessible. The three Decision
repeats share their upstream reports. Reference accounts use fractional
shares and do not establish actual fills or capital-constrained returns.

Priority work is to authenticate price and news availability, then test
BICL on unseen cutoffs with independently regenerated upstream reports.
Matched interventions should separate regime matching, prior and target
tasks, student inference and the utility gate. A same-cohort
teacher--student comparison should measure error and inference cost.
Prospective economic evaluation needs a model of lots, fills, capital
and settlement.
