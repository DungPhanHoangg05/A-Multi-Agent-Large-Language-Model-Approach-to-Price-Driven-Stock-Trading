\section*{Data and Code Availability}
Source code is available in the \href{https://github.com/DungPhanHoangg05/A-Multi-Agent-Large-Language-Model-Approach-to-Price-Driven-Stock-Trading-in-the-Vietnamese-Stock-Ma}{project repository}. Stored research inputs include price and news snapshots, prompts, charts, model responses, execution records and pinned dependencies. Supplements S1--S4 contain stock-level classification, repeated decisions, numerical selector controls and temporal sensitivity, respectively; additional historical-context comparisons appear in \ref{app:additional} and the supplementary results.

The complete data archive is currently maintained privately; public distribution depends on confirming the relevant rights and assigning a persistent identifier. Historical inputs were reconstructed for research and have not been independently authenticated at their original availability times. Saved-prediction replay reproduces the reported calculations, while newly hosted inference may differ because served model revisions are not immutable.
