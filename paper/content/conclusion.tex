\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our system adds OHLCV alpha and Vietnamese news sentiment to the price-driven QuantAgent architecture. The primary paired evaluation measures the combined augmentation on the same forecasts and execution schedule. The BICL extensions separately test the contribution of eligible historical context.

On 102 paired observations, Our system reaches \FullBA\% BA and QuantAgent \BaseBA\%. Both identify seven UP outcomes, while Our system makes fewer false LONG forecasts. Mean net account returns are \FullReturn\% and \BaseReturn\%, respectively. This is evidence of better balanced classification on this panel, with a slightly worse recorded account return and uncertainty about the separate contributions of alpha and sentiment. In the repaired secondary study, BICL v1--v4 have small mean-return differences versus Our system (Common) on complete accounts. BICL v5 has a lower mean return versus both augmented-system controls. The full-cohort repaired estimates remain unavailable.

Information integration changes recorded decisions, but the evidence does not establish stable predictive superiority, causal component value or deployable profitability. Limited dates, native within-history noncausality, uncertain news validity and support-dependent action reuse constrain interpretation. Prospective experiments with causal inputs, broader temporal coverage, repeated upstream realizations and targeted component interventions are needed to assess generalisation.
